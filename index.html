HOW TO USE THIS GENERATOR

1. Cover Page Template

Choose a template: Click any of the 13 completely different layouts. The live preview below updates instantly and the PDF uses the same style.

2. Visual Branding (Logo & Watermark)

Upload Logo: Click "Choose File" under Upload Logo. It is placed at the top and as a subtle watermark.

Tip: Use a high-quality .png or .jpg for the best result.

3. Institutional Information

University Name: Enter the full, official name of your institution.

Heading: ASSIGNMENT, LAB REPORT, THESIS, or PROJECT REPORT.

4. Course & Assignment Details

Course Code, Submission Date, Course Title, Topic: Fill these in. The date is formatted as DD-MM-YYYY.

5. SUBMITTED BY / SUBMITTED TO

  • Student: Name, ID, Department, Semester.
  • Teacher: Name, Designation, Department.

6. The Final Step: Download

  • Review all the text for spelling errors.
  • Click GENERATE & DOWNLOAD PDF.
  • The file is saved as CoverPage_[YourID].pdf.
